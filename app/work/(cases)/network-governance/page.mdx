## Overview

A proof of concept for a Fortune 100 financial-services client: a platform to govern a large enterprise network. I ran a UX audit of the existing experience, then led the redesign around three verbs — **Monitor, Resolve, Prove** — and built the Asset Map feature as a working front-end prototype on the client's own design system.

<Callout>
  Every screen should answer one question: what's wrong, how do I fix it, or how do I prove it's fixed.
